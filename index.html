Make yours

Draw your own illustration in this style, with any coding agent that runs skills: Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI and the like.

  1. Install the skill, once
    npx skills add thiagoandrade-tomtom/tomtom-docs-illustrations
  2. Say what it is for, its shape and colour
  3. Paste this into your agent

You get a live page, and SVG and PNG in your format, colour style and theme. The SVG pastes into Figma as vectors.
